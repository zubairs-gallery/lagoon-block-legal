Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-19. Applies to the Lagoon Block app for Android (package com.xubair.lagoonblock).

Published by Xubair (“we”).

Lagoon Block is a simple game. It has no accounts and no analytics of our own, and we do not sell your data. This page explains what the app does collect, through the two services it uses: Google AdMob for ads and RevenueCat for the Remove ads purchase.

What stays on your device

  • Your game progress, settings and best score are saved only on your device.
  • The app also keeps a few small values on the device to run ads fairly: when it was installed, how many games you have finished and when an ad last appeared, plus a saved note that you bought Remove ads, so ads stay off while you are offline.
  • We never receive any of this, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • If Android's backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this local data in your Google backup and restore it when you set up a new device or reinstall the app. That backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. You can watch an optional ad to revive a game or to get an extra undo. A full-screen ad can appear when a game ends, unless you bought Remove ads. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent ad fraud, Google's SDK in the app collects and uses:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, which is also used to work out your approximate location (for example your country or region),
  • device information (for example the device model and operating system version),
  • ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped,
  • performance diagnostics from the ad software (for example app launch time and responsiveness).

This data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Your consent choice

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) before ads are set up. Your choice is stored on your device and Google's SDK follows it. If the app shows Privacy options in Settings, you can use it to review or change your choice at any time. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings under Google, then Ads.

Purchases (RevenueCat and Google Play)

Remove ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. To confirm that you own it, and to restore it on a new device or after a reinstall, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives information including:

  • your purchase receipt from Google Play,
  • an anonymous app user ID that RevenueCat generates,
  • your IP address,
  • app and device information, such as the operating system version, the app version and your country or language setting.

It does not receive your name or email address from us. Google Play handles your payment details, and we never see them. RevenueCat processes this data under its privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • There are no accounts, and you do not sign in.
  • The app has no analytics of our own and no tracking of our own. The only data collected is what the two services above need.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Children

Lagoon Block is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and provided information, contact us and we will help.

Your rights and requests

You can delete all local game data yourself, at any time, by clearing the app's data in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app. The app has no account and we run no server of our own, so we hold no data about you. Data held by Google (ads) and RevenueCat (purchases) is handled under their own privacy policies, linked above. If you have questions, write to zubair812@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact
